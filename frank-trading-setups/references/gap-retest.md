# Positive-Catalyst Gap-Up Retest and Hold


### Pattern

A stock gaps up on genuine positive information, then pulls back as early longs take profit. If buyers defend the gap zone and the gap does not break, enter near the gap support.

This setup can appear more frequently than the long-base breakout and often has cleaner stop placement.

### Valid Catalysts

Prefer fundamental or narrative-changing events:
- Earnings beat / guidance raise.
- Major partnership or customer win.
- Product/technology breakthrough.
- Regulatory/policy tailwind.
- Strategic transaction or credible business model re-rating.

Weak catalysts or pure rumor gaps deserve lower grades.

### Behavioral Logic

- A real positive catalyst can reprice fundamentals.
- The pre-catalyst price/gap lower edge becomes a strong reference point: buyers believe the stock should not trade below the old price unless the catalyst is invalidated.
- Profit-taking after the gap creates the pullback; strong hands defending the gap confirms demand.
- The clean invalidation creates high win-rate / favorable risk-reward when entered near the gap.

### Key Levels

Mark the gap precisely:
- **Gap lower edge**: prior day high / pre-news upper boundary.
- **Gap upper edge**: gap-day open or lower part of the first post-news range.
- Some names may hold around the middle of the gap, but the upper/lower edges matter most.

### Entry

- Wait for pullback into/near the gap zone.
- Enter only after clear stabilization: wick rejection, reclaim, higher low, reversal candle, VWAP reclaim, or failed breakdown.
- Best entry is near the gap edge where stop is tight.
- Do not chase far above the gap if risk/reward deteriorates.

### Options Expression

For high-conviction equity long setups, consider **9–12 month calls/call spreads**, because gap retests can be choppy and may repeatedly test the gap before resolving. Avoid short-dated options unless explicitly day/swing trading with tight risk.

### Invalidation / Stop

- Exit if price breaks the **gap lower edge with volume**.
- If price only briefly undercuts and immediately reclaims, reassess; do not mechanically stop on every tiny wick unless position size/option structure requires it.
- If the catalyst is later invalidated by new negative information, setup is dead even if the chart has not fully broken.

### Target / Exit

- Primary target: prior post-gap high / recent swing high.
- Take most profit near the prior high if momentum stalls.
- Leave a small runner only if it can break and hold above the prior high.

### Pattern Anchors

Use these examples as anchors, not live recommendations:
- SNOW, BWXT: earnings gap-up, pullback, gap defended.
- CNC: guidance-related gap, gap defended.
- PATH: partnership-related gap; old price zone becomes important support.
- UNH: if price reaches gap upper edge without new catalyst, short-term longs may need to de-risk.


## Setup grading


Grade A/B/C/Reject.

### A Setup

- Catalyst or base is obvious and institutionally relevant.
- Key level is clean and widely visible.
- Entry is close to invalidation.
- Volume/price action confirms demand for longs or supply for shorts.
- Market/sector backdrop does not fight the trade.
- Risk-reward >= 2:1 and preferably 3:1.

### B Setup

- Valid structure but one flaw: weaker catalyst, imperfect level, neutral market, or late entry.
- Tradeable with smaller size or waiting for confirmation.

### C Setup

- Idea is plausible but too messy, late, or low-conviction.
- Needs better location or more confirmation.

### Reject

- Breakout/gap lacks volume or real catalyst.
- Price fails back into the old range or breaks the gap lower edge with volume.
- Risk/reward is poor.
- Major resistance is directly overhead.
- Binary event risk is unmanaged.
