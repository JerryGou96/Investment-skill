# Frank Trading Setups

Source: Frank Trading's 2025-10-19 Substack framework, with local implementation notes. Preserve attribution; do not reproduce the paid article verbatim.

## Choose the pattern

- Multi-week/month sideways base followed by a volume breakout or breakdown: read [references/range-breakout.md](references/range-breakout.md). This absorbs the former `volume-range-breakout-setup` skill, including long/short scoring, targets and risk controls.
- Positive news causes a gap up, followed by a defended retest: read [references/gap-retest.md](references/gap-retest.md).
- If the request compares both patterns, read both references. Otherwise load only the relevant one. Keep long-base breakouts distinct from the 1–3 minute post-earnings opening-range strategy.

## Shared process

Identify timeframe, fresh price/volume evidence, catalyst and market/sector context. Grade A/B/C/Reject using the selected pattern's rubric. A setup needs definable invalidation and at least 2:1 prospective reward/risk; 3:1 is preferred when realistic. Do not force a trade when the structure is unclear or the entry is late. Treat the thresholds as this framework's criteria, not empirically guaranteed outcomes.

## Output

- Setup verdict: A/B/C/Reject; range breakout / gap retest / no setup.
- Direction: long / short / wait; gap-up retest is the bullish pattern.
- Evidence: structure, volume/catalyst, key levels and market context.
- Conditional plan: entry, stop/invalidation, targets, R:R and position-risk note.
- Options tenor only if relevant to the user's request; label the framework preference.
- What would change the verdict, including a specific no-trade condition.

Use current sourced data or user-supplied charts, label missing evidence, and distinguish hypothetical levels from live quotes. Producing a plan does not place orders.
